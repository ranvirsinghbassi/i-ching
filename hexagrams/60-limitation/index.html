60. Limitation

Jié

Judgment

Necessary boundaries and restraint, like the fixed banks that give a river its direction and power. Limits used wisely create structure; used excessively, they become confinement.

The Image

Water above the lake: the image of a boundary that gives shape and containment to what it holds.

Guidance

Setting clear, reasonable limits right now — on your time, resources, or commitments — will serve you better than trying to do everything. Just avoid limits so strict they become their own burden.
