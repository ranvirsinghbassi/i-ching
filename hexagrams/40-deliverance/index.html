40. Deliverance

Xiè

Judgment

Release from tension after a period of difficulty — like a storm finally breaking. The moment calls for prompt, decisive action to resolve what remains before it settles again.

The Image

Thunder and rain set in: the image of built-up tension finally releasing.

Guidance

The pressure is starting to lift. Use this window to resolve what remains decisively and to forgive what's passed — don't let old tension linger once the storm has actually broken.
