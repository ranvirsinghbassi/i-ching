21. Biting Through

Shì Kè

Judgment

Removing an obstacle that is actively blocking progress, decisively and through legitimate means — like biting through something stuck between the teeth.

The Image

Thunder and lightning: the image of clear, decisive force used to resolve an obstruction.

Guidance

Something is standing in the way and needs to be dealt with directly, not avoided. Address it firmly and fairly, and the path forward will open.
