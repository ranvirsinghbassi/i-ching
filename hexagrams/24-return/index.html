24. Return

Fù

Judgment

The turning point — after a period of decline, a single new, positive impulse re-emerges at the very bottom, marking the start of renewal.

Guidance

A difficult cycle is beginning to turn. The change may be small at first, but it's real — nurture it gently rather than forcing a full recovery all at once.
