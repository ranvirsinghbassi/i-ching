64 Hexagrams, 4,096 Possible Readings

Every reading starts from one of these 64 hexagrams, but no two readings are really the same. Each of the six lines in a cast can land as a stable Yin, a stable Yang, or a "changing" line actively transforming into its opposite - four possible states per line. Across six lines, that's 46 = 4,096 distinct combinations: the same 64 starting hexagrams, each carrying one of 64 different patterns of changing lines.

Where This Site Goes Further

Most references stop at the traditional text for whichever combination you land on. The Book of Changes goes a step further: it reads your specific changing lines alongside the actual question you asked, and interprets the two together - not a static definition pulled off a shelf, but a reading built around your exact situation.
