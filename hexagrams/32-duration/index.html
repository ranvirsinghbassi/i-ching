32. Duration

Héng

Judgment

Endurance built on consistency of character, not rigidity. What lasts does so because its underlying principle stays constant even as its outward expression adapts.

The Image

Thunder and wind together: the image of steady, mutually reinforcing movement that renews itself.

Guidance

Whatever you're building now, focus on making its foundation consistent rather than perfect. Durability comes from steady character over time, not from a single dramatic effort.
