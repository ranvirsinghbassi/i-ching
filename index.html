Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Book of Changes?

The Book of Changes is a free online I Ching oracle. Cast a reading, ask a real question, and receive a unique interpretation that blends the traditional wisdom of the hexagrams with guidance tailored to exactly what you asked.

What is the I Ching?

The I Ching, or "Book of Changes," is one of the oldest texts in the world, dating back over 3,000 years to ancient China. It's built from 64 hexagrams — six-line figures that each describe a distinct pattern of change — prized for centuries as a mirror for reflection rather than simple fortune-telling.

How does this online oracle work?

Traditionally, a reading is cast by tossing three coins six times, once per line, from the bottom up. Each toss produces a Yin or Yang line, and sometimes a "changing line" — a moment of transformation. This oracle recreates that exact method, then interprets the resulting hexagram for the specific question you asked.

Is this I Ching oracle free to use?

Yes. Guests can cast a limited number of free readings, and creating a free account unlocks unlimited readings along with a saved history of your past consultations.

What are hexagrams and changing lines?

A hexagram is a stack of six lines, each either Yin (broken) or Yang (solid), formed by casting coins six times. Some lines are "changing" — meaning they are transforming into their opposite — which points to a second, resulting hexagram describing where the situation is heading.

How is this different from a fortune teller?

The I Ching doesn't predict a fixed future — it describes the energy of your present situation and offers guidance on how to navigate it wisely. Think of it less as prophecy and more as a structured way to reflect on your question from a new angle.

Do I need to create an account to get a reading?

No, you can consult the oracle as a guest. Creating a free account simply removes the reading limit and saves your history so you can revisit past consultations.
